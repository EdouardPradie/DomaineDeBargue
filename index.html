About Our Domain

A Legacy of Excellence

Domaine de Bargue stands as a testament to centuries of winemaking tradition and dedication. Nestled in the heart of France's most prestigious wine country, our domain combines time-honored vineyard practices with modern sustainability principles.

Each bottle tells a story of passion, craftsmanship, and respect for the land. We invite you to discover the unique character of our wines and the warmth of our hospitality.

  • ✓ Family-owned since 1890
  • ✓ Organic and sustainable practices
  • ✓ Award-winning wines
  • ✓ Exceptional hospitality
